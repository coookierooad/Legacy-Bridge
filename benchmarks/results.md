# Historical experiment notes
Reported internal synthetic CRM experiments, not independently reproduced. A legacy customer archive interface contained ten records and seven fields. A separate target CRM accepted migrated records. First successful record: 31 minutes including debugging and human guidance. Second: 8 minutes. Relative elapsed-time reduction: (31 - 8) / 31 = 74.2%. A third record was reportedly filled in 15 minutes and verified by 18 minutes. Later execution reported four target records present and verified, with six remaining when the user stopped. The latest run newly migrated one record; full-batch efficiency and automatic completion were not established. No raw logs, manual baseline, token counts or controlled comparison are available. Do not attribute the reduction solely to the framework.

## Reproduction plan
Use synthetic fixtures and a clean target. Record host, tool availability, task scope, prior knowledge, human interventions, elapsed time, tool and screenshot counts, verified objects and errors. Compare cold and warm runs under matched conditions. Test independent audit with known discrepancies and recovery with an interrupted or uncertain submission. Keep results separate from planned tests.
