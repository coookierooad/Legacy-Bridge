# Safety and data handling
Use only authorized systems and evidence channels. GUI-only tasks exclude source inspection, hidden DOM and direct business APIs, including historical evidence obtained through prohibited channels. Do not bypass screen locks or permissions. Audit is read-only. Verify writes and uncertain commits before retrying. Obtain authorization for destructive changes and rollback. Honor stop immediately and require renewed authorization to resume. Minimize retained data; avoid secrets and customer records in shared experience or public logs. Use synthetic data for public demos.
